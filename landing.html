01 / about

Tester first, engineer always.

I have spent close to five years breaking systems on purpose: finding where they slow down, where they fall over, and why. Performance testing taught me to think in systems, not just in test cases.

Now I am learning to build what I used to only break: automation frameworks, API test suites, CI pipelines. I write about all of it here, in public, so the learning sticks and maybe helps someone on the same path.
